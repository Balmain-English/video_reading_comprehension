Discussion 1

Reading Formats

Do you prefer reading physical printed books or reading on digital screens (phones or tablets)? How does your concentration change between the two?

Useful phrases: "I prefer printed books because...", "When reading on screens, I tend to skim...", "I get easily distracted by notifications..."

Keywords: concentration, distraction, tactile feel, screen fatigue, convenience.

Discussion 2

Encouraging Reading

The video mentions that deep reading builds critical thinking. How can parents and teachers encourage young people to read more books in today's digital world?

Useful phrases: "Adults should lead by example...", "Setting daily screen-free time could help...", "Introducing exciting storybooks early..."

Keywords: lead by example, screen time limits, daily habit, curiosity, immersion.

Speaking • Reflection

Debriefing: Reading in a Digital World

Discuss the questions in pairs or small groups. Give reasons and examples, and refer to ideas from the video where possible.

1. Your reading habits

When do you read deeply, and when do you skim? What helps you concentrate on a text?

2. Paper or screen?

Do you understand and remember information better on paper or on a screen? Does the type of text make a difference?

3. Reading and empathy

Can a novel help us understand people whose lives are very different from our own? Explain your answer.

4. Children and technology

What is a sensible way for parents and teachers to balance screen use and reading? Should the same rules apply to everyone?

5. Critical thinking and society

How can deep reading help people recognise misinformation and make informed decisions?

Final reflection

Has the video changed the way you think about reading? Name one idea you agree or disagree with, and explain why.

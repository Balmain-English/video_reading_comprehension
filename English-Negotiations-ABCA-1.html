Section 1: Vocabulary Check

Learn these key words from the video before watching. Then complete Exercises A and B.
1. Negotiation: A business conversation to get an agreement.
2. Small talk: Friendly, informal conversation before business.
3. Proposal: A formal plan or offer in business.
4. Win-win: A result where both sides are happy.
5. Get down to business: To start talking about work.
6. Testing the water: Exploring options without committing.
7. Run it past the board: Ask company managers for permission.
8. Priority: The most important goal.

3. Role-Play Activity (Speaking Pair Work)

Work with a partner: Student A is a seller visiting Student B's office.

  • Step 1: Greet each other and ask 2 small talk questions (e.g., flight, hotel).
  • Step 2: Transition to work ("Shall we get down to business?").
  • Step 3: Give an opening statement ("I'd like to begin by saying...").
